SCHOOL PROJECT

Website Documentation

Discover the planning, teamwork, design, coding, and testing behind the RavensTech portfolio website.

01

PLANNING & SETUP

Initial Planning & Setup

We prepared our workspace and organized the files needed for our final programming project. Our goal was to create a portfolio website where we could introduce ourselves, share our skills and backgrounds, and showcase our work as future engineers.

Behind the Blueprint

Every project begins with a plan. We organized our workspace, prepared the necessary files, and outlined the sections of RavensTech. This gave us a clear starting point for building a portfolio that presents our team, skills, backgrounds, and work as future engineers.

02

TEAM COLLABORATION

Collaborative Brainstorming & Coding

We shared ideas, discussed the design, and worked together while writing our code. Each member contributed suggestions and helped plan the website's different sections to keep everything organized and aligned with our goals.

Where Ideas Connect

Collaboration helped turn individual ideas into one shared vision. We exchanged suggestions, discussed design choices, and worked together while coding. By contributing our thoughts and helping solve problems, we shaped a website that reflects our team's goals.

03

INTERFACE PREVIEW

Front-End Showcase & Interface Preview

We previewed the website to review its appearance and progress. It features our personal information, skills, recent works, and project showcases. Seeing our ideas come together helped us evaluate the design as we moved closer to completion.

A First Look at RavensTech

With the main interface taking shape, we previewed how visitors would experience our website. We reviewed the personal introductions, skills, recent works, and project showcases, checking how each section connected to create a consistent and organized layout.

04

CODING & DEVELOPMENT

Source Code Development

We developed RavensTech using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. We worked on the website's structure, visual design, and interactive features to make it more attractive, organized, and user-friendly.

Under the Hood

Behind the interface are three essential technologies. HTML structures the content, CSS creates the visual design and responsive layout, and JavaScript powers interactive features. By combining these technologies, we transformed our plans and designs into a working RavensTech website.

05

TESTING & IMPROVEMENT

Final Testing & Development

We tested RavensTech to check its appearance, responsiveness, and functionality on different screen sizes. We made final adjustments and improvements to ensure the website worked properly and was ready for submission.

The Final Polish

Before submission, we reviewed RavensTech across different screen sizes and tested its appearance, responsiveness, and interactive features. We identified issues, made adjustments, and refined the website to improve its usability and reliability. These final checks helped prepare our project for presentation.
